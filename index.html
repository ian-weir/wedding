Frequently Asked Questions

Can we bring children?
Any other occasion we would love to spend time with you and your kids but we ask that they stay home this time.
What's the dress code?
The dress code is semi-formal. Skirt & a blouse, dress or jumpsuit for women. Dress pants, collared shirt and optionally a tie and suit jacket.
If you're unsure, feel free to reach out to us anytime.
Is there parking at the venue?
Yes! There's paid parking right behind Bonterra. It's an Indigo parking lot and should be ~$10 from 4pm until 8am the next day.
Alternatively there's lots of street parking around the venue which is free after 6pm.
If you're a cool person who takes public transit you can take the CTrain to 7th or 8th street station and walk down 8th street to the venue.
The 2, 6, 7, 13 and 90 bus routes all stop within a few blocks as well.
Can I take pictures/videos during the ceremony?
We'll have a professional photographer for the day so we'd ask that you please keep your phone away during the ceremony. We'll be sure to give access to all the photos once we have them.
